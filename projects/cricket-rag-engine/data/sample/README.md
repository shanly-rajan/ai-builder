# Sample corpus policy

Only small synthetic documents or documents with clear redistribution permission
belong in this directory. Every future fixture should record:

- title and original source;
- author or publisher;
- license or permission basis;
- retrieval date when sourced externally;
- whether its events, teams, people, and statistics are fictional.

Downloaded working documents belong in `data/raw/`, and generated chunks or indexes
belong in `data/processed/` or `data/index/`. Those directories are intentionally
ignored and must not be used as a back door for committing private or copyrighted
corpora.
