ScholarPath under the hood

A multi-agent research relay

LangChain structures model interactions. LangGraph orchestrates the workflow. LangSmith traces every step.

  1. 1Candidate Intake AgentStructures the research profile and loads Candidate-scoped preferences.
  2. 2Research Planning AgentOpenAI returns a typed SearchPlan with purposeful queries.
  3. 3Supervisor Discovery AgentYou.com searches first; deterministic rules retain plausible people and provenance.

↪ On timeout, retryable error, or too few useful profiles: route to Tavily, preserve partial results, then continue.

  1. 4Evidence Verification AgentTavily retrieves pages; OpenAI extracts source-linked claims without inventing facts.
  2. 5Research Fit Evaluation AgentOpenAI applies the 100-point rubric and cites supporting evidence IDs.
  3. 6Independent Review AgentNebius accepts or recommends a grounded revision.
  4. 7Shortlist Synthesis AgentDeterministically ranks up to five Verified Supervisors as proposed.
  5. 8Candidate Review GateApprove exact IDs, reject with reasons, or request more research.

↩ Rejection or revised preferences flow through the Preference Learning Agent into Mem0, then back to planning. Approval alone unlocks shortlist persistence and the final briefing.

Models propose. Deterministic code validates and routes. The Candidate decides.
