Integration boundaries

O

OpenAI

Typed planning, claim extraction, and Research Fit components. No autonomous browsing.

Y

You.com

Primary web search; one query at a time with limits and timeout.

T

Tavily

Bounded fallback search and known-page content extraction.

N

Nebius

Independent Research Fit review; no tools, browsing, or new evidence.

M

Mem0

Candidate-scoped durable preferences; writes follow explicit actions only.

L

LangSmith

Optional redacted traces and regression evaluation—not a factual source.

Deterministic control plane

Python owns routing, retry limits, deduplication, arithmetic, lifecycle transitions, reconciliation, ranking, and approval enforcement.

✓

Evidence before claims

Each factual claim retains its source, retrieval time, confidence, support flag, and conflicts.

↯

Fail safely

Typed errors, timeouts, bounded retries, partial-result preservation, and finite loops.

≠

Availability stays separate

Never inferred, never hidden inside Research Fit, and never converted into admission probability.

◆

Candidate controls writes

Explicit approval is mandatory before persistent shortlist state; outreach is not implemented.
